Risk Tracker is a crypto futures tracking concept focused on making portfolio exposure, position performance and risk signals easier to understand. The goal: make risk visible before it becomes a surprise.

About this project
Purpose and scope
✳

A workspace concept for crypto futures tracking, built around a compact overview, position book and risk-oriented analytics. This UI prototype emphasizes readable financial data, explicit risk levels and responsive navigation.
